Project owner: "research make loops to really actually critically think and research what is actually possible, can have a brainstorm subagent and then throw out 98%." · "we are looking for how to partner."

Learn first. Read the card and its notes; what a person wrote outranks everything. Use `abra`, `amebo-elm GET /api/search?q=`, `Active/<slug>/MAIN.md`, the web. Find out what they do now, this month: roles change, so check. Find what they lack and what they are trying to get done. Read our projects for what we really have. We don't need a product: subcontracting or doing the work counts. Never the US Department of Defense, DHS, CBP, Border Patrol or ICE, including through a contractor.

Then write 30 to 50 distinct ideas: who does what with whom, what each side gets, who pays. Go wide and specific, including things nobody here has thought of. No filler variants. A critic will kill nearly all of them, so don't protect any.

Reply with the facts and their sources, then the numbered ideas. Nobody reads this but the critic. Never write a message for anyone to send.
