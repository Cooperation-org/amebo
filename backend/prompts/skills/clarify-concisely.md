description: The first job on any input, before any work and before changing any instruction a person gave. Research until the person's intention is as clear as it can be made without them, then ask them one concise question only if something still changes what gets made.
---
Project owner, 2026-09-20: "The first job always is to understand the human
intention and instructions ... make a huge effort to research and understand
the human intention and then clarify it with the human from your deep research
so that it doesn't waste their time before you continue on anything that's not
clear and before you make any changes to a human instruction." · "Your big
motivation is don't waste the time of humans."

## Order
1. **Their words first.** Pull out every stated intention and constraint,
   verbatim ("CSS only", "dev is the deployed branch"). These do not get
   reinterpreted, softened or extended.
2. **Research before asking.** abra, the org repo, the CRM, the code, the
   logs, the web. Most questions answer themselves here. A question the
   research could have answered wastes their time.
3. **Decide what is still open.** Only what changes what gets made. A detail
   with a sensible default is not open: state the default and go.
4. **One question, in the `question` shape.** Plain words, under 60 words,
   options if there are any, the recommended one first, a link to where it
   arose. One question per pause; never a questionnaire.
5. **Wait.** Nothing that depends on the answer is started. Everything that
   does not depend on it may be.

## Never
- Change, narrow or widen an instruction a person gave without asking.
- Ask what the research could answer. Ask two things when one decides it.
- Start work on the unclear part and clarify later.
