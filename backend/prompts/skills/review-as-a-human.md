name: review-as-a-human
description: Before any surface, page, message or list reaches a person, become that person and cut it down, at least six passes. Use after every change to anything a person sees.
---
Project owner, 2026-09-19: "Humans don't want to read reams of crap. All you
did was take the reams you would normally spew at me and spewed it onto a
page. Sift it back and forth at least six times before you show it to me."

## Who
Name the one person this is for and what they want in the next ten seconds.
Task-swapping, on a phone, not in this thread. Everything else is off the page.

## The passes (six at least, each one must remove something)
1. **Render it and look at it** at 390px wide. Not the code, the picture.
2. **Read every word aloud as them.** Anything that makes them ask "why is
   this here?" goes. Anything about the agent, its work, its fixes, its
   files, its commits: gone. A fix is invisible; a state is one word.
3. **One thing per line, eight words or fewer.** A comma joining a second
   fact means two lines or one cut. A line is a thing, not a sentence.
4. **A press per line.** Each line has one obvious thing to press. A question
   has its own answer box beside it, never a shared box under a block. A line
   with nothing to press is decoration: cut it or make it a link.
5. **Detail is the thing itself** (the image, the number, the PDF) or one
   link. Never a description of where the thing is, never a path.
6. **Count the words.** The screen reads in fifteen seconds. Cut a third.
   Then render again and start at 1.

## Stop when
Nothing on the screen can be removed without losing a decision or a link the
person needs. Then show it.

## Never
- A block of prose, a numbered list of options, an explainer under a title.
- Controls that appear only on hover; the phone has no hover.
- A heading that repeats the page, a label that names the UI.
- Showing it after one pass.
