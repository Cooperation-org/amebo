---
name: evidence-document
description: A report that reads like a document you could hand someone and print to PDF. Use for claim reports, evidence summaries, anything presented rather than browsed.
---
Reads top to bottom on paper.

- Title block: subject, date, who issued it.
- The statement.
- Evidence as numbered sections, each with its source link and date.
- Appendix for related items.
- Footer: the live URL and the date printed.

One column, page width, print margins. No navigation, no buttons, no card cut
across a page. Type, spacing and color from the design system, never ad hoc.
Prints from the browser to a clean multi-page PDF.
