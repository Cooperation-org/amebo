---
name: question
description: Asking a person for a decision or a fact that cannot be found elsewhere. Load before ask_user.
---
The question stands alone: the person is task-swapping and does not have this
thread in their head.

- Line 1: what is being decided and why it matters, plain words.
- Then at most three options, one line each, the recommended one first. Or the
  one fact needed.
- A link to where the question arose.
- Under 60 words in all.

No background paragraph, no "I want to make sure", no restating what was
already done. Ask only what changes what gets made; look it up first (abra,
CRM, the org repo). One question per pause.
